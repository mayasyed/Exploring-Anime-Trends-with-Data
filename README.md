# Anime 101: Using Data to Find My First Anime

I’ve never watched anime before, but my partner loves it. Instead of relying on memes or chaotic clips online, I took a data-driven approach to decide where I should begin. I analysed over 12,000 anime titles to understand what’s popular, what’s highly rated, and what’s beginner-friendly.

## Questions I Explored
- What genres are most popular?
- Do people prefer short or long anime?
- What are the highest-rated short anime for first-timers?

## Tools Used
- Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn)
- Jupyter Notebook
- GitHub

## Outcome
I created a shortlist of anime series I’d actually be willing to try, based entirely on the data!
